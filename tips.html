Travel Tips

Prepare before you leave.

Good trips start with simple habits: plan your budget, pack only what you need, and keep important information easy to access.

Packing List

  • Passport or national ID.
  • Phone charger and power bank.
  • Comfortable shoes.
  • Reusable water bottle.

Budget Steps

  1. Set a maximum spending amount.
  2. Separate hotel, transport, food, and activity costs.
  3. Keep emergency cash available.
  4. Track spending daily.

Safety Notes

  • Share your plan with someone you trust.
  • Save hotel and emergency numbers offline.
  • Use official taxis or public transport apps.
  • Keep copies of important documents.

Quick Advice

Start with walkable plans.

For short student trips, choose destinations where the hotel, food, transportation, and activities are close together. This saves time and makes the trip easier to manage.
